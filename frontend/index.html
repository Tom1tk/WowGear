          New to Classic? Words on this page
          BiS
          "Best in slot": the best item for that equipment slot.
          BoP / BoE
          Binds when picked up (only you can use it) / binds when equipped (you can buy or sell it on the Auction House).
          Elite
          A strong monster (gold dragon on its portrait). Usually needs a group.
          Instance / dungeon
          A private area for your group of 5 (raids: 20 or 40 players).
          Trash
          The normal monsters in a dungeon, not the bosses.
          Attunement
          A quest or item you need before you can enter some raids.
          Reputation
          Your standing with a faction. Higher standing opens more rewards.
          Pre-raid
          Gear from level-60 dungeons, quests, crafting and reputation — before raiding.
          Score
          How good an item is for your spec, from stat weights. Higher is better. Compare only within one slot.
