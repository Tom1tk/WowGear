      Upgrading & currencies (Midnight S2)

      • Mistcrests upgrade gear along its track (Adventurer → Veteran → Champion → Hero → Myth; Myth caps at ilvl 334). Each rank costs 20 crests; the weekly cap starts low and rises by 100 per week per tier. Visit the upgrade vendor Cuzolth in Silvermoon City (48.6, 62); Vaskarn beside him trades higher crests down a tier.
      • Nebulous Voidcores are bonus rolls: a Great Vault reward option when you fill 3+ slots (up to one per week), and one per dungeon keystone or raid boss (1 Voidcore per raid roll in S2) for a guaranteed Myth-track item from that loot pool.
      • Fill the Great Vault every week — Heroic-raid vault rewards now roll Myth 1/6 (ilvl 318) — and convert tier pieces at the Catalyst (catalyzed pieces keep their stats and cantrips). Myth-track gear comes from Mythic raid and +10 keystones.

      Sources: Icy Veins Gear Upgrading Guide, Icy Veins Mythic+ Season 2 Guide, Icy Veins Great Vault Guide.
