        Glossary of terms on this page
        BiS
        "Best in slot": the best item for that equipment slot.
        BoP / BoE
        Binds when picked up (only you can use it) / binds when equipped (you can buy or sell it on the Auction House).
        Elite
        A strong monster (gold dragon on its portrait). Usually needs a group.
        Instance / dungeon
        A private area for your group of 5 (raids: 10, 20 or 40 players).
        Trash
        The normal monsters in a dungeon, not the bosses.
        Forever
        WoW Forever: the Classic-based game where level 60 is the permanent cap and new content (dungeons, zones, the Skyborne race) is added over time.
        Skyborne
        The new playable race in Forever. It starts on Zephras Isle.
        Reputation
        Your standing with a faction. Higher standing opens more rewards.
        Pre-raid
        Gear from dungeons, quests, crafting and reputation — before raiding.
        Raid tier
        A set of raids that open together. The first Forever raids open on 9 December 2026.
        Uncertain / 1 source
        We compare several item databases. "Uncertain" means they disagree (or the loot is datamined and may change). "1 source" means only one had the item.
        Score
        How good an item is for your spec, from stat weights. Higher is better. Compare only within one slot.

        Uncertain: our data sources disagree; hover it or open the row to see why. “1 source” (in an opened row) means only one database has the item. The gold bar is the percent score gained over what you wear; “new” means nothing useful is worn there. A * after a score means some stats on the item are not counted.
