Pick a character first: look one up by name, or choose race, class, spec and level. This panel then lists the best item for each slot, how it scores for your spec, where to get it, and how much it beats what you wear.

How the numbers work

Score
Every stat on an item is multiplied by what it is worth to your spec and added up, counted in attack power (spell power or healing for casters). For an Arms Warrior 1 Strength is worth about 2.2 attack power, so +10 Strength adds 22. Weapons also count their damage per second. Levels 10–59 use levelling weights, level 60 uses endgame weights. Scores only compare within one slot: a ring scores lower than a chest piece because it has fewer stats.
You wear
The same score for the item you have on now, so the two numbers line up.
Gain
How much better the best item is than yours: (best − yours) ÷ yours. A best item of 30 against your 20 is +50%. “new” means you wear nothing useful there. A * after a score means some of the item's stats are not counted.
Glossary of terms on this page
BiS
"Best in slot": the best item for that equipment slot.
BoP / BoE
Binds when picked up (only you can use it) / binds when equipped (you can buy or sell it on the Auction House).
Elite
A strong monster (gold dragon on its portrait). Usually needs a group.
Instance / dungeon
A private area for your group of 5 (raids: 10, 20 or 40 players).
Trash
The normal monsters in a dungeon, not the bosses.
Forever
WoW Forever: the Classic-based game where level 60 is the permanent cap and new content (dungeons, zones, the Skyborne race) is added over time.
Skyborne
The new playable race in Forever. It starts on Zephras Isle.
Reputation
Your standing with a faction. Higher standing opens more rewards.
Pre-raid
Gear from dungeons, quests, crafting and reputation — before raiding.
Raid tier
A set of raids that open together. The first Forever raids open on 9 December 2026.
Uncertain / 1 source
We compare several item databases. "Uncertain" means they disagree (or the loot is datamined and may change). "1 source" means only one had the item.
Score
How good an item is for your spec, from stat weights. Higher is better. Compare only within one slot.
Gain
How much better the best item is than what you wear, in percent.
