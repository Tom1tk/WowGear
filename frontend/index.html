      Upgrading & currencies (Midnight S1)

      • Dawncrests upgrade gear along its track (Adventurer → Veteran → Champion → Hero → Myth; Myth caps at ilvl 289). Each rank costs 20 crests, so a full 1→6 upgrade is 100 — the weekly cap. Visit the upgrade vendor Cuzolth in Silvermoon City (48.6, 62).
      • Ascendant Voidcores add bonus ranks beyond the normal cap to weapons and trinkets on the Hero/Myth track (crafted included) — save them for your BiS weapon, then trinkets.
      • Nebulous Voidcores — 2 per week — are bonus rolls: one per dungeon keystone, two per raid encounter, for a guaranteed Hero/Myth-track item from that loot pool (already-obtained items are removed from future rolls).
      • Fill the Great Vault every week and convert tier pieces at the Catalyst; Myth-track gear comes from Mythic raid and +10 keystones.

      Sources: Icy Veins Gear Upgrading Guide, Brewmaster BiS guide, Warcraft Wiki.
